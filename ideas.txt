-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHAT WOULD GENUINELY SET THIS APART
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Ranked by credit earned versus effort. None of these are safe to build TODAY.
This is the "after the event" list - except #1 and #6, which are worth starting
tonight while everything is still fresh.


IDEA 1 - LIVE ARRIVAL CHECK-IN + OCCUPANCY HEATMAP          [highest impact]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Ushers tap a seat as each guest arrives. The organiser's map fills up in real
time, with a live counter: "412 of 611 seated".

Why it matters:
  - Nobody expects this at a convocation. It is the single most impressive
    thing you could add.
  - It gives you an exact attendance record the moment the ceremony ends.
  - It is a natural fit: the seat map and the attendee model already exist.

Feeds directly into Idea 6.


IDEA 2 - DIRECTIONS, NOT JUST A SEAT NUMBER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Turn "Q21" into:
    "Gate-2  ->  up the left aisle  ->  row Q  ->  6th seat from the aisle"

Every guest bringing an elderly parent will remember this. All the geometry
needed to compute it already exists in src/data/defaultSeatingData.ts (block,
row, column, aisle position and recommended gate are all stored per seat).


IDEA 3 - THE TICKET AS A KEEPSAKE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
All 171 students uploaded a passport-size photograph in the registration form.

A pass carrying the graduate's photograph and name is something the family
keeps. A plain stub is something they drop at the door. Same printing cost.


IDEA 4 - "WHERE IS MY FAMILY SITTING?"   [PARTLY BUILT ALREADY]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Verified live on 15.09.2026: searching "SHARMISTHA ROUT" on the production
kiosk already returns B27 (hers) together with T6 and T5 - her father's and
mother's seats. The family is found by one search.

So the hard part is done. What is left is presentation: label the results
explicitly ("Your seat" / "Your father" / "Your mother") instead of leaving
the guest to work out which seat is whose. That is a small, high-payoff
change.


IDEA 5 - OFFLINE-FIRST KIOSK
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
There is currently no service worker. If the hall wifi drops, the kiosk is a
blank page.

One service worker makes it work with no network at all. Unglamorous - and
exactly what separates a real product from a demo.


IDEA 6 - POST-EVENT REPORT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
A single printable page for the committee the next morning:
    - attendance by section (MBBS, Nursing, PG, faculty, parents)
    - no-shows
    - gate throughput
    - Exam Hall usage

This is the artefact that makes YOU look professional long after the hall has
emptied. Pairs with Idea 1, which supplies the data.


OTHER IDEAS WORTH CONSIDERING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Accessibility lane: wheelchair spaces already exist (A11, A12). Add a
    flagged "assistance required" list so ushers know in advance.
  - Audit trail: who changed which seat and when. Makes the committee trust
    the system.
  - Big-screen lobby display: rotating "find your seat" prompt plus a large
    QR code, auto-refreshing.
